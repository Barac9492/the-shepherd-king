# Bethlehem art review assets

## Status
BLOCKED on visual acceptance. This is an isolated local art review, not the playable episode, a released game, or reference-quality completion. Final independent visual review: improved face/hands/pose; remaining primitive-looking legs, rigid sleeve/elbow wraps, tube-like tunic and belt. Three bounded author/review cycles ended without a visual pass. Do not integrate or publish on the strength of structural tests.

## Assets
- `courtyard.glb`: original deterministic Blender 5.2.2 scene authored by `author-bethlehem-courtyard.py`. 24,163 triangles; 1,215,428 bytes. No downloaded environment models/textures.
- `warrior.glb`: original Blender 5.2.2 character authored by `author-bethlehem-character.py`. One 34-joint skin; six clips: Idle, Walk, Crouch, Carry, DrawWater, Pour. This is the intermediate character, not the default review asset.
- `warrior-refined.glb`: derivative of the original warrior plus MakeHuman CC0 head, distal forearm and hand surfaces, topology-fitted hair and original procedural eyes/wraps. Generated by `refine-bethlehem-warrior.mjs`. Original animation JSON and skin hierarchy preserved. See `warrior-refinement-inventory.json` for hashes, exact sources and limitations.
- The `.blend` originals precede the Node-based CC0 surface repair. They are not falsely represented as source files containing the final repaired anatomy.
- `src/bethlehem-art-pose.js` applies art-lab-only arm IK and prop placement over the clips at runtime. These contact corrections are not baked into the GLBs or Blender originals, and are not a claim of gameplay-ready interactions or verified grips/foot contacts.
