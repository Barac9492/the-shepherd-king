# 다윗의 땅 · 거처 아크 (Base-Building Arc Plan)

Status: plan. Act 1 (아둘람) is the only act being built now. Each later act starts only after Ethan gives Act 1 a fun verdict on his phone.

## One-line hook

You build a base out of people, and you keep having to leave it. At the end, God tells you that you are not the one who builds the house.

## Core rule across every act

- The main resource is **people**, not stone or wood. Everyone who arrives brings a need. Once that need is met, they can take a role, and their role takes work off David's hands. The people you served start serving the next arrivals.
- **Growth makes you visible.** More people means Saul (later, others) hears about you sooner (1 Sam 22:6). Watchers buy time; nothing buys permanence.
- **No raiding as an economy.** Resources come from water, bread, flocks, work and gifts. David's raids from Ziklag and his lie to Achish (1 Sam 27:8–11) are not turned into a reward loop.
- Every act ends with a scripture card that separates **what the text records** from **what the game imagined**, using 개역한글 text from the Korean Bible Society reader.
- One act = one 2–4 minute session on a phone. Its own save key. Never touches story-mode progress.

## Act 1 · 아둘람 (built in this branch)

**Loop, about 3 minutes**

1. Start alone at the cave. Bread basket holds the 5 loaves David asked for at Nob (삼상 21:3). Spring is downhill.
2. People arrive along the road, each with one visible need: 물, 떡, or 불 곁 자리. Their short line says which of 22:2's three groups they belong to (distress, debt, bitterness). The specific stories are imagined and labeled as such.
3. David carries water or bread to them, or leads them to the fire. Once their need is met they follow David. Walking into a work ring (샘 · 화덕 · 파수 바위) gives them that role. If David doesn't lead them anywhere, they rest in the camp and still count.
4. Roles automate: water carriers and bakers start serving new arrivals themselves; watchers slow Saul's attention.
5. Each joined group adds to "함께한 자" (target 약 400, 22:2). The camp visibly fills: tents, fires, jars, sheep.
6. Saul's attention rises with group size. When it peaks, or when time runs out, 갓 arrives with 22:5. You lead everyone down the road toward 헤렛.
7. Ending card: 22:1–2, 22:5, 시 142:4 and 142:7, plus recorded/imagined lists.

**Fun check (Ethan, phone):** Is it fun within 30–40 seconds? Does the moment when the people you served start serving others feel good? Is the "you have to leave" ending meaningful rather than annoying?

**Not in Act 1:** combat, Saul's soldiers on the map, saving between sessions beyond the final count, English text.

## Guardrails

- Biblical claims come from cited verses. Locations, layouts, individual names and backstories are imagined and labeled.
- Ps 142's title says "다윗이 굴에 있을 때"; the text does not say which cave. The card says this.
- Keep it non-violent and suitable for a church youth audience.
- An AI visual pass is not acceptance. Ethan's verdict on his phone is the gate before Act 2.
