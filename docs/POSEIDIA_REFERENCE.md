# Visual reference: Poseidia

User-supplied reference: https://poseidia.vercel.app/
Inspected directly in browser on 2026-09-25 (Asia/Seoul): title scene, sea approach, automated voyage into the city, bow/chase camera control and help screen.

## Direction for the next Shepherd King mockup

The user rejected shader/color variations and asked for genuinely different graphics implementation approaches, then supplied Poseidia as an additional reference. Treat it as a reference, not approval of every effect or a mandate to copy its world.

- World-scale composition: a strong distant landmark, large readable architectural silhouettes, layered foreground/midground/background.
- Build an inhabitable place, not a display room of unrelated assets. Repeating architecture should establish a coherent spatial rhythm and destination.
- Atmosphere: water reflections, haze, changing light and a clear near/far contrast make simple forms feel like a large world.
- Camera: reveal locations gradually through an approach and a purposeful route; preserve player agency rather than turning the game into an automatic tour.
- Presentation: quiet typography and small contextual HUD, leaving the world prominent.
- Our stone-corridor mockup proves a skinned asset and color/depth caching pipeline; it does NOT meet this environmental composition/atmosphere bar.

## Application candidates, not approved changes

- Bethlehem: valley-scale composition, layered hills and a legible distant village, rather than an isolated pen on generic terrain.
- En Gedi: cliff/cave destination, strong scale contrast and light/shadow transitions.
- Palace: an approach through courtyard/colonnade into the narrative space, with a coherent lighting and architectural vocabulary.

## Boundaries

- Poseidia's Atlantis, crystal and esoteric narrative are not references for biblical content.
- Characters are small in the inspected city views. This does not validate our close-up facial quality, cloth deformation or hand interaction.
- Do not remove controls, chapter logic, mobile support or free movement merely to imitate its automated voyage.
- Public site availability is not a reuse license. Do not copy its code or assets without confirming rights.
- No assertion of its exact rendering algorithms, asset provenance, or phone performance has been established by visual inspection.
- No production/gameplay changes were made in this reference review.
